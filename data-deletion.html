User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Users may request deletion of data associated with their authorization or use of XiaoZheng Threads Radar.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Revoke XiaoZheng Threads Radar's access through the applicable Threads or Meta account permissions.
  2. Contact the application owner using the contact information registered for XiaoZheng Threads Radar in Meta for Developers.
  3. State that you are requesting deletion of data associated with your XiaoZheng Threads Radar authorization.

What Will Be Deleted

Where applicable, stored authentication-related information and application-managed data associated with the requesting user will be deleted, subject to legal or platform obligations requiring limited retention.

Processing Time

Valid deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period after the request is verified.
